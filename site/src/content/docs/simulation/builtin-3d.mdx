The built-in 3D **Test world** (`horus/world/`) runs natively on Linux, Windows, and macOS with real **ArduCopter 4.7.1 SITL**, requiring zero Docker, Gazebo, or GStreamer setup.

## Choosing a test world

HORUS enforces **one active Test world at a time**. Choose the backend that matches your goal:

| World | Setup | OS Support | Fidelity & Maps | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Built-in 3D** (`horus/world/`) | Zero extra setup (`horus`) | Linux, Windows, macOS | MuJoCo 3D SAR scenes, 3D spectator view, vector/plaza maps | Daily development, Fault Lab, CI, laptops |
| **Gazebo Harmonic** (`sim/`) | Docker + GStreamer | Linux (local or remote Koko) | Full physics, civilian traffic, procedural worldgen (`baseline`/`chaotic`) | Dense outdoor evaluation sweeps & ROS/Gazebo stacks |
| **GTA V** (`gta/`) | Windows GTA V Enhanced + mod | Windows host + Linux/WSL bridge | Photorealistic city/countryside + whole-map tiles (250 m / 1 km / 4 km) | Multi-kilometer urban navigation & street-map missions |

## Included scenes

| Scene | Environment | Included targets & features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`indoor_sar`** *(default)* | Enclosed SAR room with partition wall, doorway, and north corridor | Furniture, AprilTag `tag36h11` ID 0 landing pad at home, standing/sitting/lying/walking people in distinct shirt colors |
| **`forest_sar`** | Outdoor woodland clearing and forest trail | Collision tree trunks and canopies, hikers, and vector map data |
| **`urban_plaza`** | City blocks, Central Plaza, and fountain | Parked vehicle, pedestrians, and tiled street/place map (`find_place`, `get_area_map`) |
| **`procedural`** | Seeded outdoor world | Procedurally generated trees, landmarks, hikers, and map data from a chosen seed |

## Physics, sensors & Fault Lab

- **JSON lockstep physics:** Implements **ArduPilot**'s UDP `9002` binary servo-frame decoder and JSON state encoder over MuJoCo rigid-body and contact physics with an F450 thrust/drag model.
- **7-beam ToF & stabilized gimbal:** Sends 6 horizontal sector beams + 1 downward `range_down` (`DISTANCE_SENSOR`) at 20 Hz, and drives a 2-axis stabilized MAVLink gimbal (`pitch_deg`, `yaw_deg`).
- **3D Spectator View & Fault Lab:** Switch between Follow, Overhead, and Orbit 3D camera views inside **HORUS Studio**, inject `0 ms`–`5000 ms` AI-link round-trip latency, or click **Cut AI Link** to observe the `EXECUTING → HOLDING → auto_rth` failsafe live.
