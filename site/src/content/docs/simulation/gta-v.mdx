GTA V (`gta/`) runs as a physical **Test world** with collisions enabled (`HorusGta.dll` + `horus_capture.asi`).

## How it connects

- **JSON lockstep:** ArduCopter SITL (`--model JSON`) exchanges UDP `9002` servo/state packets with `HorusGta.dll`.
- **7-beam ToF & gimbal:** `HorusGta.dll` casts 7 collision rays bridged by `horus.tof_bridge.gta` into `DISTANCE_SENSOR`, and `horus-gta-gimbal` (`horus/gimbal/gta.py`) relays horizon-locked pitch/yaw setpoints.
- **Whole-map tiles:** Built once with `scripts/maps/build_gta_tiles.py` and served at 3 levels of detail (250 m, 1000 m, 4000 m) to both the **AI model** (`find_place`, `get_area_map`) and the **Control app** map.
- **Attaching from HORUS Studio:** Select **Settings → Hardware & External Sim → GTA** and provide the running **Drone board** URL and bearer token.
