---

## 1. Launch HORUS Studio

```sh
horus
```

HORUS Studio starts on `127.0.0.1:8765`, prints its token-protected URL (`http://127.0.0.1:8765/#token=...`), and opens your default browser.

## 2. Launch a Built-in 3D World

On the **Launchpad**, if SITL or ONNX models are not cached yet, click **Download SITL & Vision Models**. Then click **Launch Indoor SAR Room** (or **Outdoor Forest**, **Urban Plaza**, **Procedural World**).

HORUS Studio automatically:
1. Starts the MuJoCo **Test world** with 7-beam ToF sensors, 2-axis gimbal, and `sim://` camera stream.
2. Starts **ArduPilot** (`arducopter --model JSON`) in an isolated run directory.
3. Starts the **Drone board** (`horus mcp`) and binds both the **AI pilot** and passive **Control app** display reader.

## 3. Fly manually or with AI

- **Without an AI key:** Open **Fault Lab → Manual Flight & Vision** to test `Take Off 2m`, directional nudges, gimbal `Look`, and open-vocabulary terms immediately.
- **With an AI model:** Click **Settings → AI Pilot & Crew** to pick OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama, Claude Code, or OMP, then click any curated mission chip in **Mission Composer**.
- **Emergencies:** The pinned **`HOLD` / `LAND` / `HOME`** buttons always bypass the **AI pilot** and lock out AI flight tools until your next mission.
