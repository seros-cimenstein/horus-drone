The app in one paragraph: a web page on the laptop at `http://127.0.0.1:8765/`, served by the AI

news feed, live camera with the numbered objects (about 1 Hz), drone state, map, and always-visible
HOLD / LAND / HOME buttons that go to `POST /emergency` and never through the AI. Laptop only. The
page's reads of the drone board are passive: they never count as AI contact and never replace the
AI's last set-of-mark view.
